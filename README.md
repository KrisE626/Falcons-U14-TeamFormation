# Shorelake U14 Positions

An interactive guide for U14 recreational boys: a 3-5-2 that becomes a 5-3-2 without the ball, with a No. 9 (target) and No. 10 (second striker) up top.

- Tap any player for what they do with and without the ball, phrases to say, and questions to ask (answers hidden until tapped).
- Toggle between 3-5-2 and 5-3-2 to watch the wing-backs and top two shift.
- Quiz mode: "who am I?" with 11 questions.

Single file, no build step, no dependencies: everything lives in `index.html`.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages

1. Create a new **public** repository (for example `shorelake-u14-positions`).
2. Upload `index.html` and `README.md` (Add file, then Upload files, then Commit).
3. Go to **Settings, then Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, Branch to **main** and folder to **/ (root)**, then Save.
4. After a minute the site is live at `https://<username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

To change anything, edit `index.html` in the repo and commit. The site updates on its own.
