Ideas for the whole team

One team, two shapes

With the ball we spread out as a 3-5-2: the wing-backs push up and wide. When we lose it, they sprint back and we become a 5-3-2. Use the toggle above to watch it happen.

The 9 and 10 work as a pair

The 9 is the target: high, strong on the ball, always looking to finish. The 10 is the link: dropping into gaps, turning, and setting up the 9. They should be close enough to help each other, but never in the same spot.

Stay connected

Every player should be a good pass away from a teammate. Gaps between the defenders, midfielders and strikers are where the other team scores.

The five seconds after the ball changes hands

Lose it: get between the ball and our goal. Win it: look forward fast, before the other team is set.

Talk

Names, not just noise. Try “Man on!”, “Time!”, “Turn!”, “Switch!” and “Keeper!”.

Recreational reminder: kids will play different spots as the season goes on. The goal is understanding every job, not locking anyone into one. Wing-back is the most tiring position, so rotate it.
